# Code of Conduct

This applies to every repository under [mazino2d](https://github.com/mazino2d) that does not have its own code of conduct: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and explain why when they do.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block anyone from the project, temporarily or permanently.

To report a problem, use **Report content** on the comment or issue, or contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/mazino2d). Reports are kept confidential.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
